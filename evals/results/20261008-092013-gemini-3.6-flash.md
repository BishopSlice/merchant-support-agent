## How these are measured

- **Resolution rate:** of cases that are plain data fixes (easy fixes, multi-issue, warnings, must-not-hand-off and angry merchants), the share that passed every check: no handoff, a re-check after the last fix with the issue gone, required text and citations present, forbidden text absent.
- **Handoff precision and recall:** a handoff is any case saved during the conversation. Precision: of conversations that handed off, the share that should have. Recall: of conversations that should have, the share that did.
- **Wrong advice and case completeness:** graded by a second model call against the rubrics in `evals/rubrics/`.
- **Cost:** token counts times the prices in `merchant_agent.config.MODEL_PRICES`.
