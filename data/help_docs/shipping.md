In the United States and many other countries, products need shipping information, including cost and delivery speed. Products with missing or wrong shipping information can be disapproved.

The recommended way is to set up shipping in your Merchant Center account settings, which then applies to your products. Use the shipping value in your product data only to override those settings for specific products.

Delivery speed is made of two parts: handling time, from the order until it is handed to the carrier, and transit time, from the carrier to the customer.
