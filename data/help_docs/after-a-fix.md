Product issues only affect the products involved. Account issues affect every product in the account. Product issues are listed on the Needs attention tab under Products, and account issues show in a banner at the top of Merchant Center.

To fix a product issue, correct the product data and upload it again. Some issues also offer a suggested automatic fix in Merchant Center.

After you update your products, Google checks them again. These checks usually finish within 24 to 48 hours, and products show as pending while they are reviewed.
