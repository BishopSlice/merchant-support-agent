Merchant Center can try to fix product images that were disapproved because of promotional overlays. It removes text, watermarks, overlays or logos it detects in the image.

Automatic image improvements are off by default. You can turn them on under Products, on the Automations tab.

They may not fix every image. Merchant Center does not try when the result would be poor, for example complex multi-panel collages, heavy text over the main product, or strong or dense watermarks. In those cases, upload an image that follows the image requirements.

Image improvements work on the overlays in an existing image. A missing image or a placeholder needs a real image of the product, added by you.
