Every product needs a main image link. Without one the product cannot show.

The link must be a full web address that starts with http:// or https://.

The image must show the actual product. Placeholder images, generic images and graphics that do not show the product are not allowed.

Images must be at least 500 by 500 pixels, and around 1500 by 1500 pixels or larger is recommended. Do not add promotional text, logos, watermarks or other overlays to the main image.
