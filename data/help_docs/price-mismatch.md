title: Price in the feed does not match the website

The price you send must match the price shown on the product's page and at checkout, in the same currency. If they differ, Google disapproves the product.

Common causes are a delay between changing the price on your website and updating your product data, a sale price with the wrong dates, or price markup on the page that differs from the price shoppers see.

To fix it, change the price in your product data so it matches the product page exactly, then upload the corrected data. You can edit products one at a time in Merchant Center or download the list of affected products and fix them in bulk.

After the fix, Google's crawler rechecks disapproved products over the next few hours or days. Once the prices match, the product is approved again.

To stop it happening again, update your product data on a regular schedule so prices change in both places at the same time.
