The availability you send (in stock, out of stock, preorder or backorder) must match what the product page and checkout show. If they differ, Google disapproves the product.

This often happens because of a delay between changing stock on your website and updating your product data.

To fix it, change the availability in your product data to match the product page, then upload the corrected data. You can edit products one at a time in Merchant Center or download the list of affected products and fix them in bulk.

After the fix, it can take some time before the product's status updates. Google may take up to 12 hours to review your website, and once the values match the product shows again.
