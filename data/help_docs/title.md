Every product needs a title, and it can be at most 150 characters long.

A good title says clearly what the product is, using specific words. Include the brand and key details such as size, color and material, especially when they tell variants apart.

Do not put prices, shipping details or promotional text in the title. Avoid all capital letters, symbols, HTML and extra spaces.

To fix a title that is too long, shorten it to 150 characters or less and keep the most important details near the start.
