Google's list of unapproved pharmaceuticals and supplements includes cannabidiol (CBD). Products on this list are not allowed in Shopping ads or free listings.

This is a policy decision, not a data problem, so it cannot be fixed by editing product details. Whether a specific product is allowed is decided by Google's policy team under the healthcare and medicines policy.
