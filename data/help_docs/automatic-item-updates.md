Merchant Center can update some product information automatically, using what it finds on your website. Automatic item updates cover price, sale price, availability and condition.

It works by reading the structured data (schema.org markup) on your product pages, and other information on the page. If your product data says one thing and the website says another, for example $4 in your data but $3 on the page, the product is updated to match the website. This helps avoid some price and availability mismatches.

Automatic item updates are on by default. You can turn them on or off under Products, on the Automations tab, by choosing an automation and opening View details.

Automatic updates are not a replacement for keeping your product data up to date. They are meant for occasional mismatches on a small share of products. If a price or availability changes often, for example more than once a day, the product may be disapproved instead of updated.

Updates may also not work if your product pages have no structured data, or the markup is incomplete or incorrect. If a page shows several strikethrough prices, the right one may not be found. For sale prices, check that the sale price effective date and its time zone are correct. Updates can also stop if the number of mismatches becomes too large.
